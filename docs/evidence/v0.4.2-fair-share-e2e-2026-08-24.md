## Real-subscription E2E

The owner explicitly authorized real quota consumption. The installed Relay
app-server was exercised directly with read-only sandboxing and
`approvalPolicy=never`. Each prompt requested one exact, minimal reply.
Official Codex remained running. The managed Relay UI was temporarily stopped
so only one multiplexer could write the Relay state, then relaunched after the
probe.

Before the probe, the scheduler history showed three full-quota workers with
zero dispatches, one 96–97% worker with three dispatches, and one 26% worker
with five dispatches.

| Probe | Persisted worker | Protocol terminal | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New task `01a033c7-13eb…` | Subscription 2 | `turn/completed` | PASS |
| New task `01a033c7-8a77…` | Subscription 4 | `turn/completed` | PASS |
| New task `01a033c7-c8c8…` | Subscription 5 | `turn/completed` | PASS |

After those three probes, Subscription 2, 4 and 5 had each advanced from zero
to one dispatch. The 26% worker remained at five dispatches and was not reused
while unused full-quota workers existed.

A post-E2E review found that the older weighted-deficit credit would otherwise
have made the 26% worker the next candidate after the unused workers each
received one turn. The candidate was tightened to normalized service
(`dispatches / current quota weight`) and the full release gates were rerun.
Production preview then changed from the 26% worker with five dispatches to
Subscription 2 at 100% with one dispatch. A fourth real new-task probe
`01a033ce-e03b…` persisted Subscription 2 as `worker_selected`, completed the
turn, and advanced only that worker from one to two dispatches. The 26% worker
remained at five, proving the corrected choice was not preview-only.

The first task was then resumed for a second real turn with a short Goal:

- transactional handoff phases: `PREPARED → COPIED → RESUMED → COMMITTED`;
- source: Subscription 2, generation 1;
- target: Subscription 3, generation 2;
- reason: `handoff_balanced_boundary`;
- second turn: accepted and `turn/completed`;
- Goal after handoff: present, status `active`, objective matched exactly.

This is evidence that new tasks distribute across previously unused workers and
that an existing task plus Goal can cross a completed-turn boundary without
losing canonical history. It does not claim that five upstream subscriptions
become one OpenAI billing account, nor that Relay can safely interrupt and
replay a turn after file/tool side effects.

## Depleted-worker scope

The deterministic integration suite covers a confirmed 0% worker and verifies
that it is excluded. No live subscription was deliberately burned down or its
persisted quota falsified merely to manufacture a 0% production condition.
The live pool instead proved the safe observable case available at test time:
a 26% worker was not selected while unused 100% workers were eligible.
