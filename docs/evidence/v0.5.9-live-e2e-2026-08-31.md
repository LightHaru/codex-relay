## Resilience and safety boundaries

Deterministic restart, lease heartbeat/expiry, duplicate-turn single-flight,
temporary 502 rotation, SSE idle recovery, cancellation-after-output recovery,
and no-replay-after-commit tests all passed. A post-output recovery terminal is
an intentional safety stop: it prevents duplicate tool side effects and does
not expose a public worker/account switch.

The following required live transitions remain **LIVE PENDING / NOT PROVEN**
because no active source returned a genuine quota rejection during this run:

- real A→B, B→C, or C→D quota rejection while preserving one turn;
- a long chat crossing a real quota boundary;
- live archived-chat continuation and live existing-chat canonical resume;
- tool/approval side-effect boundary under a real provider failure;
- all sources genuinely depleted and the resulting single pool-level error.

It would be unsafe to manufacture those states by falsifying persisted quota or
draining every account. The deterministic fixtures cover the transition logic
until a naturally depleted, authorized source is available.
